You can access and use <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> to proactively identify and resolve application performance issues before they impact your end users. This guide walks you through how to access <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT>, view the account-wide overview, analyze and get recommendations for specific risks, update detection thresholds, and use the tool within New Relic MCP server. For more information about what <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> is and its key benefits, refer to [introduction to <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT>](/docs/errors-inbox/performance-risks/introduction).

## Acceder a la bandeja de entrada de riesgos de rendimiento [#access]

You can access <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> in two ways: at the entity level, scoped to a single entity or workload, or at the account level, covering every entity in your account.

* [Access at entity level](#access-entity)
* [Access at account level](#access-account)

### Access at entity level [#access-entity]

At the entity level, <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> shows performance issues for a single entity&apos;s or workload&apos;s telemetry data. This is available for:

* Servicios de APM
* Aplicación Browser
* Carga de trabajo

**Para acceder a <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> de una entidad:**

1. Go to <DNT>[one.newrelic.com &gt; All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities)</DNT>, then select <DNT>**APM &amp; Services**</DNT>, <DNT>**Browser**</DNT>, or <DNT>**Workloads**</DNT>.
2. Select the required entity or workload.
3. In the navigation pane, under the <DNT>**Triage**</DNT> section, click <DNT>**Performance Risks**</DNT>.

### Access at account level [#access-account]

At the account level, <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> is available in two views: an account-wide dashboard, and a list of performance risk groups scoped to a workload or entity that you choose.

**Para acceder a <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> para una cuenta:**

1. Vaya a <DNT>[one.newrelic.com &gt; All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) &gt; Errors Inbox</DNT>.
2. In the entity navigation pane, under the <DNT>**Performance Risks**</DNT> section, click <DNT>**Performance Overview**</DNT> for the account-wide dashboard (see [View the Performance overview](#overview)), or <DNT>**Risk Groups**</DNT> for a scoped list.
3. In <DNT>**Risk Groups**</DNT>, use the scope selector to choose <DNT>**Workload**</DNT> or <DNT>**Entities**</DNT>, then select the specific [workload](/docs/new-relic-solutions/new-relic-one/workloads/workloads-overview) or entity you want to review, or use the search box to find it by name or description.
4. Optionally, add more filters, such as by <DNT>**Performance risk group metadata state**</DNT> (for example, <DNT>Unresolved</DNT>), and use <DNT>**Sort by**</DNT> to change the order of results.

## Ver y analizar riesgos de rendimiento [#view-analyze]

Una vez que acceda a <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT>, puede ver los problemas detectados de dos maneras: la vista <DNT>**Triage**</DNT> y la vista <DNT>**Group performance risks**</DNT>. Ambas vistas muestran los mismos datos subyacentes pero ofrecen diferentes formas de analizarlos.

### Triaje [#triage]

La vista <DNT>**Triage**</DNT> procesa y agrupa automáticamente riesgos de rendimiento similares. Las consultas similares se calculan, almacenan y combinan en un solo grupo de riesgo de rendimiento. Por ejemplo, múltiples consultas que comparten el mismo patrón —como variaciones de una cláusula `IN` — se agrupan en una sola entrada en lugar de aparecer como elementos separados. Esto facilita identificar y priorizar los problemas de mayor impacto sin revisar cada ocurrencia individualmente.

Use the <DNT>**Triage**</DNT> view when you want a pre-grouped summary of performance issues to identify patterns across your application.

<img title="Screenshot of the Triage view in Performance Risks inbox showing pre-grouped performance issues." alt="Screenshot of the Triage view in Performance Risks inbox showing pre-grouped performance issues." src="/images/performancerisks_triage.webp" />

### Riesgos de rendimiento del grupo [#group]

The <DNT>**Group performance risks**</DNT> view displays the raw performance risk data without pre-computed groupings. Normal query representations (such as comma and question mark placeholders) are shown, and you can filter and group the data based on different attributes, such as:

* El usuario afectado por el riesgo de rendimiento
* El servicio que genera las llamadas
* La API que desencadena las consultas

Utilice la vista <DNT>**Group performance risks**</DNT> cuando necesite analizar riesgos de rendimiento basados en atributos específicos que sean relevantes para su investigación.

<img title="Screenshot of the Group performance risks view in Performance Risks inbox showing raw performance risk data." alt="Screenshot of the Group performance risks view in Performance Risks inbox showing raw performance risk data." src="/images/performancerisks_grpperrisk.webp" />

## Get recommendations [#recommendations]

From the <DNT>**Triage**</DNT> view, you can generate AI-powered recommendations for a specific performance risk instead of investigating its occurrences manually.

**To get recommendations for a performance risk:**

1. From the <DNT>**Triage**</DNT> view, open a performance risk that has multiple occurrences.
2. Haga clic en <DNT>**Generate insights**</DNT>.

<DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> analyzes the occurrences for that performance risk and returns a hypothesis about the root cause, along with recommended next steps to resolve it. The exact recommendations vary depending on the performance risk detected.

<img title="Sample performance risk recommendations." alt="Sample performance risk recommendations." src="/images/PRinsights.webp" />

<Callout variant="important">
  New Relic enables AI features by default for all accounts. If you don&apos;t see this option, your account may not have it enabled. Contact your organization admin to request access.
</Callout>

If a recommendation suggests adjusting a threshold, see [Set the thresholds](#set-thresholds).

## Establecer los umbrales [#set-thresholds]

<DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> uses configurable thresholds to automatically detect performance anomalies. You can update these thresholds at both the account and entity levels — including for [workloads](/docs/new-relic-solutions/new-relic-one/workloads/workloads-overview) — to ensure that the detection criteria align with your specific performance monitoring needs and priorities. These thresholds determine what constitutes a performance risk and help ensure <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> alerts you to the most relevant and actionable performance issues in your applications.

Puede configurar los umbrales de Riesgos de rendimiento a nivel de cuenta o entidad:

* [Umbrales a nivel de cuenta](#account-thresholds)
* [Umbrales a nivel de entidad](#entity-thresholds)

### Configurar los umbrales a nivel de cuenta [#account-thresholds]

Configurar umbrales a nivel de cuenta le permite establecer criterios predeterminados de detección de riesgos de rendimiento que se aplican a todas las entidades dentro de la cuenta. Esto es útil para mantener estándares de monitoreo consistentes y garantizar que todas las aplicaciones sean evaluadas frente a los mismos puntos de referencia de rendimiento.

**Para configurar los umbrales a nivel de cuenta:**

<CollapserGroup>
  <Collapser id="new-account-thresholds" title="Nuevos umbrales a nivel de cuenta">
    1. Vaya a <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com &gt; All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) &gt; Errors Inbox &gt; Performance Thresholds**.</DNT>.
    2. En la pestaña **Accounts**, haga clic en <DNT>**+ New**</DNT>.
    3. En el menú desplegable <DNT>**Account**</DNT>, seleccione la cuenta para la que desea crear un nuevo umbral.
    4. Establezca los límites requeridos para cada analizador.
    5. Haga clic en <DNT>**Submit**</DNT> para guardar.
  </Collapser>

  <Collapser id="edit-account-thresholds" title="Editar umbrales existentes a nivel de cuenta">
    1. Vaya a <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com &gt; All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) &gt; Errors Inbox &gt; Performance Thresholds**.</DNT>.
    2. En la pestaña **Accounts**, haz clic en el botón <DNT>**Edit**</DNT> junto a la cuenta que deseas actualizar.
    3. Establezca los límites requeridos para cada analizador.
    4. Haga clic en <DNT>**Submit**</DNT> para guardar.

    <Callout variant="tip">
      When you modify thresholds, New Relic displays your username to track who made the changes.
    </Callout>
  </Collapser>
</CollapserGroup>

### Establecer los umbrales a nivel de entidad [#entity-thresholds]

Configurar umbrales a nivel de entidad le permite personalizar los criterios de detección de riesgos de rendimiento para aplicaciones o servicios específicos que pueden tener características o requisitos de rendimiento únicos. Esto es particularmente útil para adaptar el monitoreo a diferentes tipos de aplicaciones, entornos o prioridades de negocio.

**Para establecer los umbrales a nivel de entidad:**

Puede configurar los umbrales para una entidad específica de una de las siguientes maneras:

<CollapserGroup>
  <Collapser id="entity-triage" title="Desde la página Riesgos de rendimiento">
    1. Vaya a <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com &gt; All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) &gt; APM &amp; Services**.</DNT>.
    2. Seleccione la entidad requerida.
    3. En el panel de navegación de la entidad, haga clic en <DNT>**Performance Risks**</DNT> en la sección <DNT>**Triage**</DNT>.
    4. Haga clic en <DNT>**Adjust performance thresholds**</DNT> en la esquina superior derecha.
    5. Establezca los límites requeridos para cada analizador.
    6. Haga clic en <DNT>**Submit**</DNT> para guardar.

    <Callout variant="tip">
      When you modify thresholds, New Relic displays your username to track who made the changes.
    </Callout>
  </Collapser>

  <Collapser id="entity-page" title="Desde la página Umbrales de rendimiento">
    1. Vaya a <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com &gt; All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) &gt; Errors Inbox &gt; Performance Thresholds**.</DNT>.
    2. En la pestaña <DNT>**Entities**</DNT>:
    3. Desde el menú desplegable <DNT>**Account**</DNT>, seleccione la cuenta de su entidad.
    4. Desde el menú desplegable <DNT>**Entity**</DNT>, seleccione la entidad que desea actualizar.
    5. Establezca los límites requeridos para cada analizador.
    6. Haga clic en <DNT>**Submit**</DNT> para guardar.

    <Callout variant="tip">
      When you modify thresholds, New Relic displays your username to track who made the changes.
    </Callout>
  </Collapser>
</CollapserGroup>

### View threshold lines on charts [#threshold-guidelines]

Performance risk charts display reference lines to help you interpret whether a value is within an expected range:

* **Minimum and maximum guidelines**: Calculated automatically from the historical data collected for that chart, showing the typical range of values over time.
* **Current threshold**: Reflects the threshold that&apos;s currently active for that analyzer, based on your account-level or entity-level threshold settings. If both are set, the entity-level threshold takes precedence.

Use these lines to see how close a metric is to its configured threshold, without needing to open the threshold settings.

## Utilice la herramienta de bandeja de entrada de riesgos de rendimiento en el servidor MCP de New Relic. [#using]

You can also access the <DNT>Performance Risks inbox</DNT> tool ([`list_entity_performance_risk_groups`](/docs/agentic-ai/mcp/tool-reference/#performance)) in [New Relic MCP server](/docs/agentic-ai/mcp/overview/). This tool lists all the Performance Risks detected for a given entity, along with their details such as risk type, severity, and occurrence count. You can use this tool to programmatically retrieve performance risk data.
