<DNT>Cloud Cost Intelligence</DNT> is an Advanced Compute product. Pricing is based on the volume of billing export data that your cloud provider publishes and <DNT>CCI</DNT> processes, referred to as billing file ingest.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Contact your New Relic account team for the latest pricing details or to discuss volume-based options.
</Callout>

## Billing file ingest rate

Billing file ingest rates depend on your cloud provider:

- **<DNT>AWS</DNT>**: Ingest is charged at **23 aCCUs per GB** of billing data processed.
- **<DNT>Azure</DNT>**: Ingest is charged at **690 aCCUs per GB** of billing data processed.
- **<DNT>GCP</DNT>**: Ingest is charged at **46 aCCUs per GB** of billing data processed.

## What is billing file ingest?

Billing file ingest refers to the size of the billing export files that your cloud provider (<DNT>AWS</DNT>, <DNT>Azure</DNT>, or <DNT>GCP</DNT>) publishes to your designated storage container. <DNT>Cloud Cost Intelligence</DNT> imports these files to build cost analysis and insights.

<Callout variant="important">
  Billing file ingest is different from New Relic's standard [data ingest](/docs/accounts/accounts-billing/new-relic-one-pricing-billing/data-ingest-billing/), which applies to streaming telemetry data such as metrics, logs, and traces. The two are metered and billed separately.
</Callout>

For an overview of how New Relic pricing works more broadly, see [New Relic pricing](/docs/accounts/accounts-billing/new-relic-one-pricing-billing/new-relic-one-pricing-billing/).
