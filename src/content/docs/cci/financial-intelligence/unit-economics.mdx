Cloud Cost Intelligence shows your cloud spend at a high level, such as your total **AWS** or **EC2** cost. That view tells you *how much* you spend, but not what it costs to deliver a single unit of value from your service.

Unit economics closes that gap. It measures a business metric such as **cost per query**: how much it costs your business to run one query. When a cost goes up, a unit metric helps you tell the difference between two very different situations:

- **Growth**: costs rose because you served more users or ran more queries. This usually reflects business growth.
- **Waste**: costs rose while the work stayed the same, which points to an inefficiency to investigate.

## Create a unit metric [#create-unit-metric]

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com > All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) > Cloud Cost Intelligence**</DNT>.
2. Click <DNT>**Create unit cost**</DNT> to set up a unit metric that connects your cloud spend to a unit of business value, such as a query.
3. In the <DNT>**Define New Unit Cost**</DNT> section, complete the following fields:

## Define the cost and usage [#define-cost-usage]

Cloud Cost Intelligence calculates the unit cost by dividing one value by another. You define these two parts side by side:

- <DNT>**Cost Definition**</DNT>: the cost metric you want to measure, such as your spend.
- <DNT>**Usage Definition**</DNT>: the business metric you want to measure, such as the number of queries.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Use the switch button between the two panels to swap the numerator and the denominator. When you switch, the panel headers update to show which part is the <DNT>**Value Unit (Numerator)**</DNT> and which is the <DNT>**Cost (Denominator)**</DNT>. This lets you measure either cost per unit (for example, cost per query) or units per cost (for example, the number of queries delivered for a given cost).
</Callout>

### Cost Definition [#cost-definition]

Define your cost metric using either the <DNT>**Filters**</DNT> tab or the <DNT>**NRQL**</DNT> tab.

<DNT>**Filters**</DNT>

1. Click <DNT>**Configure Filters**</DNT> and choose a filter source:
   - <DNT>**Cost overview**</DNT>: Filter by cloud provider dimensions such as account, service, and region.
   - <DNT>**Kubernetes**</DNT>: Filter by Kubernetes dimensions such as cluster, container, and deployment.
2. Click <DNT>**Validate & Calculate Spend**</DNT> to calculate the matched spend for the selected date range.
3. The <DNT>**Matched Spend**</DNT> value shows the monthly spend for your filters. Click <DNT>**Preview Spend**</DNT> to see the result.

<DNT>**NRQL**</DNT>

Use the <DNT>**NRQL**</DNT> tab to define your cost metric with a NRQL query instead of filters.

## Numerator and denominator [#numerator-denominator]

The <DNT>**Dynamic Unit Cost Calculation**</DNT> panel shows the live result as you build the metric. It divides the numerator by the denominator to produce the <DNT>**Resulting Metric**</DNT>. For example, dividing your total cost by your total number of queries gives the cost per query.

When the result looks correct, click <DNT>**Confirm**</DNT> to save the unit cost, or click <DNT>**Discard changes**</DNT> to discard it.

## View results [#view-results]

After you create a unit cost, the grid view displays all the unit metrics you've created, for example <DNT>**Cost per Transaction**</DNT>, <DNT>**Kubernetes Cost per Pod**</DNT>, and <DNT>**CCI Cost per query**</DNT>.

Click <DNT>**Details >**</DNT> on a unit metric to open its detail view, which includes:

- **Summary metrics**: <DNT>**Unit Cost**</DNT>, <DNT>**Total Spend**</DNT> (the numerator), the denominator volume (for example, <DNT>**Transaction Volume**</DNT>), and <DNT>**Efficiency Score**</DNT>. Each metric shows the percentage change compared to the previous period. The efficiency score shows a value only when benchmark data is available.
- <DNT>**Unit Cost Trend**</DNT>: a chart that plots total spend and unit cost over the selected time range.
- <DNT>**Cost Contribution to Unit Cost**</DNT>: a chart that shows the percentage of total cost per day. Use the <DNT>**Group by**</DNT> dropdown to break the contribution down by a dimension such as account.
- <DNT>**Definition**</DNT>: the <DNT>**Cost Definition**</DNT> and <DNT>**Usage Definition**</DNT> queries that define the metric.
- <DNT>**Metadata**</DNT>: details such as who created the metric, when it was created and last modified, its status, and its unit.

You can group results, for example, by cloud provider, to see how your unit cost breaks down across dimensions.
