## How do I access capabilities currently in preview? [#preview-access]

Some <DNT>CCI</DNT> capabilities, such as the <DNT>FinOps Agent</DNT> and <DNT>CCI Alerts</DNT>, are available as preview features before general availability. To enable them:

1. Log in to your New Relic account.
2. In the bottom-left corner, select your <DNT>**User Profile**</DNT> icon.
3. Go to <DNT>**Administration > Previews and Trials**</DNT>.
4. Locate the feature you want to enable:
   - <DNT>**FinOps Agent Preview:**</DNT> Toggle this on to enable natural language cost querying and advanced cloud spend analysis.
   - <DNT>**CCI Alerts Preview:**</DNT> Toggle this on to enable the ability to create and manage alerts directly within the CCI ecosystem.
5. Refresh your browser to see the new capabilities reflected in the <DNT>Ask AI</DNT> interface.
