The Flow view shows the active health status of a flow, and is designed to make it simple to identify the areas experiencing issues in the journey.

To open the Flow view, click any row of the <DNT>**Flows**</DNT> page. See [Flows page](/docs/pathpoint/get-started-pathpoint#flows-page) for more.

<Callout title="Note">
  The Flow view reflects the live health status of your signals, refreshed at the interval set for the flow.
</Callout>

Each Pathpoint flow is divided into:

<CollapserGroup>
  <Collapser
    id="kpi-bar"
    title="KPI bar"
  >
    At the top of the Flow view, the <DNT>**KPI bar**</DNT> displays the key business metrics configured for this flow, such as order fulfillment time, cart abandonment, or revenue. These give you immediate context on the business impact of what you're seeing below.

    KPIs can also be associated with individual stages. To view the KPIs for a specific stage, click the trend icon on the stage header.

    KPIs in the Flow view are displayed at the time window you configure for each KPI. For example, you can configure an "Orders Placed" KPI to show data for the last 24 hours compared to the same period a week ago, or a "Revenue" KPI to show the last week compared to the prior week. For more information, see [Add a KPI](/docs/pathpoint/kpis#add-a-kpi).
  </Collapser>

  <Collapser
    id="stages"
    title="Stages"
  >
    Stages represent the major phases of your business journey, such as Shop, Order, Notify, and Deliver. Each stage rolls up the health, congestion, throughput, and infrastructure state of all the services running beneath it, giving you a single, consolidated view of how that part of the business is performing.

    In the Flow view, stages are color-coded to reflect their current health: green (healthy), yellow (warning), or red (critical). This lets you immediately spot which phase of the journey is experiencing issues, without having to dig through individual services.

    For more on how health is calculated, see [Health indicators](/docs/pathpoint/health-indicators).
  </Collapser>

  <Collapser
    id="levels-and-steps"
    title="Levels and steps"
  >
    Steps break down each stage into the specific activities that make up that phase of the journey. Within a stage, steps are organized into numbered levels that reflect their sequential order, making it easy to understand dependencies and trace where an issue is occurring.

    Each step displays a summary of its signal health. Click any step to expand it and see the individual signals contributing to its status, along with their source type, such as an alert, workload, or APM entity.

    If a stage displays a <DNT>**Signals not reporting**</DNT> warning, some signals in that stage appear to be missing. Click <DNT>**View details**</DNT> for details.
  </Collapser>

  <Collapser
    id="signals"
    title="Signals"
  >
    Signals are the most granular layer of a flow. Each signal maps to a specific entity or alert and reflects its current health. Signals roll up into steps, which roll up into stages, so a problem at the signal level surfaces all the way up the journey.

    Signals are color-coded by health status. For more information, see [Health indicators](/docs/pathpoint/health-indicators).

    Click any signal to open its detail panel and view golden signals and open alert events, directly in context, without leaving the flow.
  </Collapser>
</CollapserGroup>

## Filtering the view [#filtering-the-view]

Use the toggles in the top right of the Flow view to focus on what matters:

* <DNT>**Expand critical only**</DNT>: Expands all steps with signals in a critical state. Warning, healthy, and unknown signals are collapsed or hidden. Steps excluded from the level status rollup are hidden even if they're in a critical state.
* <DNT>**Expand unhealthy only**</DNT>: Expands all steps with signals in a warning or critical state. Healthy and unknown signals are collapsed or hidden. Steps excluded from the level status rollup are hidden even if they're in a warning or critical state.
* <DNT>**Expand all**</DNT>: Expands all levels across all stages at once, showing all steps and signals regardless of their health state.

## Signal error indicators [#signal-error-indicators]

The signals that make up a flow can change over time. When Pathpoint detects an issue with the signals in a stage, a warning indicator appears at the top of the affected stage.

There are two signal warnings you may encounter:

* <DNT>**Missing signals**</DNT>: A signal that was included in the flow no longer exists in New Relic. Click the warning icon to see the list of missing signals for that stage. In the modal, click <DNT>**Update signals**</DNT> to open the signals overlay and make the necessary adjustments.
* <DNT>**No access**</DNT>: Because different users may have access to different data scopes, a flow may contain data from accounts you don't have access to. This indicator is purely informational, and appears at the top of the affected stage to let you know you're seeing only partial data there.

## Playback [#playback]

Playback replays a flow's health across a period of time, so you can see how it changed rather than only how it looks now. Turn on the <DNT>**Playback**</DNT> toggle at the top of a flow.

The timeline that appears works with two spans of time:

* A **window**, the whole period under review. Change it with the dropdown in the top right of the timeline. Defaults to 30 minutes.
* An **interval**, which divides that window into equal segments. Change it with the dropdown beside the play button. Defaults to 1 minute, and longer intervals aren't available for shorter windows.

Each interval appears as a block on the timeline, colored to show the flow's status during that segment. A red block means an underlying entity was alerting. Select a block to see the flow's health at that point, or click the play button to move through the window in order. A badge above the timeline shows which health type you're replaying, and the header above it shows both the window and the point you have selected.

To share a particular moment with someone, click <DNT>**Copy snapshot link**</DNT>.

### Playback limitations [#playback-limitations]

* Playback displays up to 40 items at each level of the hierarchy: 40 signals per step, 40 steps per level, 40 levels per stage, and 40 stages per flow. A step containing 60 signals shows 40 of them during Playback. This limit applies to Playback only. Outside Playback, the flow displays all its signals.
* Playback covers a rolling 30 or 90 days, viewable up to 7 days at a time. Which of the two ranges applies depends on your account's data retention period for custom events.
* A flow migrated from an earlier version of Pathpoint has Playback available from the point of migration or creation onward. Health history recorded before that point isn't available.

Pathpoint records health as custom events, so Playback reflects what a flow's structure and health actually were at each point in time. A flow that had three stages five days ago and gained a fourth on the sixth day plays back as three stages for those first five days, and four from the sixth day onward.

## Audit a change [#audit-a-change]

The audit log records who changed a flow and when. It doesn't record what changed.

To open it, select <DNT>**Audit**</DNT> from the <DNT>**...**</DNT> menu in the top right of the flow.

## View as code [#view-as-code]

You can view and manage your flows as code using NerdGraph or Terraform. To open the code preview, select <DNT>**View as code**</DNT> from the same menu. It gives you four options:

* <DNT>**Terraform**</DNT>: View the Terraform configuration for this flow. Use this to manage your flow as infrastructure-as-code and version-control your Pathpoint setup.
* <DNT>**Update with NerdGraph**</DNT>: Preview the NerdGraph mutation that updates this flow's current configuration.
* <DNT>**Create with NerdGraph**</DNT>: Preview the NerdGraph mutation that creates this flow from scratch. Use this as a starting point for automating flow creation.
* <DNT>**Query with NerdGraph**</DNT>: Preview the NerdGraph query that retrieves this flow's configuration. Useful for auditing or building integrations on top of Pathpoint data.

For the full API reference, see [NerdGraph tutorial: Create and manage Pathpoint flows](/docs/apis/nerdgraph/examples/nerdgraph-pathpoint-flows).
