The <DNT>**KPIs**</DNT> page gives you a dedicated view to analyze the business metrics configured for your flow. To access it, select <DNT>**KPIs**</DNT> from the navigation within a flow.

The KPIs page displays all KPIs configured for your flow as time series charts, giving you a deeper view into how your key business metrics are trending over time.

Each KPI chart shows a time series visualization of how the metric has trended over time. Use the time picker in the top right to adjust the time range of the chart, such as the last 30 minutes or the last hour.

KPIs are grouped by scope. <DNT>**Global KPIs**</DNT> apply to the whole flow, while stage-level KPIs are associated with a specific stage.

To manage your KPIs, click <DNT>**Manage KPIs**</DNT> in the top right.

## Manage KPIs [#manage-kpis]

The <DNT>**Manage KPIs**</DNT> page is where you create, edit, and delete the KPIs for your flow. It shows a table of all configured KPIs with their name, category, description, and associated stage.

### Add a KPI [#add-a-kpi]

You can also start from a flow's <DNT>**Summary**</DNT> tab by clicking <DNT>**+ Add KPIs**</DNT>.

To add a new KPI, fill in the following fields in the <DNT>**Add a KPI**</DNT> section:

* <DNT>**KPI name**</DNT> (required): A descriptive, business-friendly name for the metric.
* <DNT>**Category**</DNT> (optional): Classify the KPI by functional area, such as Financial Performance or Growth.
* <DNT>**KPI applies to**</DNT>: Choose whether the KPI applies to the whole flow (Global) or to a specific stage. Stage-level KPIs are associated with that stage and can be viewed by clicking the trend icon on the stage header in the Flow view.
* <DNT>**Description**</DNT> (optional): A short description of what the KPI measures.
* <DNT>**Query**</DNT>: The NRQL query used to calculate the KPI. Click <DNT>**Preview**</DNT> to validate the query and confirm it returns the expected result before saving.

Click <DNT>**Save**</DNT> to add the KPI.

### Edit, duplicate or delete a KPI [#edit-duplicate-delete-kpi]

To edit, duplicate, or delete a KPI, click the <DNT>**...**</DNT> menu on the KPI's row in the table and select <DNT>**Edit**</DNT>, <DNT>**Duplicate**</DNT>, or <DNT>**Delete**</DNT>.

<Callout variant="caution">
  Deleting a KPI is permanent and cannot be undone.
</Callout>
