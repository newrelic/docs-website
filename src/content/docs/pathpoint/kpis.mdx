This page covers Pathpoint 2.0. For the generally available version, see [Introduction to New Relic Pathpoint](/docs/new-relic-solutions/business-observability/intro-pathpoint).

The <DNT>**KPIs**</DNT> page gives you a dedicated view to analyze the business metrics configured for your flow. To access it, select <DNT>**KPIs**</DNT> from the navigation within a flow.

The KPIs page displays all KPIs configured for your flow as time series charts, giving you a deeper view into how your key business metrics are trending over time.

Each KPI chart shows a time series visualization of how the metric has trended over time. Use the time picker in the top right to adjust the time range of the chart, such as the last 30 minutes or the last hour.

KPIs are grouped by scope:

* <DNT>**Global KPIs**</DNT>: Apply to the whole flow.
* <DNT>**Stage-level KPIs**</DNT>: Apply to a specific stage.

To manage your KPIs, click <DNT>**Manage KPIs**</DNT> in the top right.

## Manage KPIs [#manage-kpis]

The <DNT>**Manage KPIs**</DNT> page is where you create, edit, and delete the KPIs for your flow. It shows a table of all configured KPIs with their name, category, description, stage, <DNT>**Metric Query**</DNT>, and <DNT>**Evaluation Window**</DNT>.

### Add a KPI [#add-a-kpi]

On a flow's <DNT>**Summary**</DNT> tab, click <DNT>**+ Add KPIs**</DNT>.

1. Enter a <DNT>**KPI name**</DNT>, and optionally a <DNT>**Category**</DNT> and <DNT>**Description**</DNT>.
2. In <DNT>**Global / Stage**</DNT>, choose the whole flow or a specific stage.
3. Build the <DNT>**Synthesis Query**</DNT>, which converts raw events into a business metric:
   * <DNT>**FROM**</DNT>: The event type.
   * <DNT>**SELECT**</DNT>: The aggregation and attribute, named with <DNT>**AS**</DNT>.
   * <DNT>**WHERE**</DNT>: Optional conditions.
4. Under <DNT>**Since and compare**</DNT>, keep <DNT>**Default**</DNT>, or select <DNT>**Custom**</DNT> and set <DNT>**Since**</DNT> and <DNT>**Compare against**</DNT>.
5. Click <DNT>**Preview**</DNT> to check the results.
6. Click <DNT>**Add a KPI**</DNT>.

<Callout title="Note">
  <DNT>**Since**</DNT> and <DNT>**Compare against**</DNT> accept relative time only, so the KPI stays current. Absolute timestamps aren't supported.

  The [Flow view](/docs/pathpoint/pathpoint-view) has no time picker. It shows current state, so <DNT>**Since and compare**</DNT> is what sets a KPI's cadence, such as week over week or month over month.
</Callout>

### Edit, duplicate or delete a KPI [#edit-duplicate-delete-kpi]

Click the <DNT>**...**</DNT> menu on a KPI's row in the table:

* <DNT>**Edit**</DNT>: Reopens the KPI so you can change any of its fields.
* <DNT>**Duplicate**</DNT>: Creates a copy to modify, useful for a variant of an existing KPI.
* <DNT>**Delete**</DNT>: Removes the KPI from the flow.

<Callout variant="caution">
  Deleting a KPI is permanent and cannot be undone.
</Callout>
