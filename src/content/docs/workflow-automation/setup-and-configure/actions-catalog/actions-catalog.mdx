The New Relic actions catalog provides pre-built actions for cloud providers, DevOps tools, ITSM platforms, and New Relic's own platform. Use these actions as steps in your workflow automations to trigger deployments, manage incidents, and automate operational tasks across your stack.

Actions follow a hierarchical naming convention: `company.product.action`
