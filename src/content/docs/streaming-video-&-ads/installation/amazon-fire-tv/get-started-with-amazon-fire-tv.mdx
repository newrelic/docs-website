Use VegaTracker to monitor video playback in your Amazon Fire TV (Vega OS) application. VegaTracker captures video events and sends them to New Relic as entities, giving you visibility into playback quality across Fire TV devices. With device info enabled, you can filter and facet your video data by real Fire TV device model and OS version in NRDB.

<Callout variant="important">
  VegaTracker sends data through New Relic's mobile pipeline. The `applicationToken` is a mobile application token — distinct from a browser license key and obtained separately from [Install the tracker](#install).
</Callout>

## Prerequisites [#prerequisites]

Make sure you have:

- A New Relic [account ID](/docs/accounts/accounts-billing/account-setup/account-id)
- Familiarity with the [Streaming Video & Ads compatibility requirements](/docs/streaming-video-&-ads/installation/compatibility-requirements)

## Install the tracker [#install]

<Steps>

<Step>
### Access New Relic

Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com](https://one.newrelic.com) > Integrations & Agents**</DNT>.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select Streaming Video & Ads

Select the <DNT>**Streaming Video & Ads**</DNT> tile.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select account

Select an <DNT>**Account**</DNT> from the dropdown and click <DNT>**Continue**</DNT>.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select platform

Select <DNT>**Amazon Vega / Fire TV**</DNT> and click <DNT>**Continue**</DNT>.
</Step>

<Step>
### Select video player

Select your video player and click <DNT>**Continue**</DNT>. VegaTracker supports Shaka Player, Dash.js, and HTML5.
</Step>

<Step>
### Name your application

Enter a name for your application and click <DNT>**Create**</DNT>. This generates the mobile application token you'll use to initialize `VegaTracker`.
</Step>

<Step>
### Complete player-specific installation

Follow the installation guide for your selected player:

<DocTiles>
  <DocTile title="Shaka Player" path="/docs/streaming-video-&-ads/installation/amazon-fire-tv/install-shaka-player">Install VegaTracker for Shaka Player.</DocTile>
  <DocTile title="Dash.js" path="/docs/streaming-video-&-ads/installation/amazon-fire-tv/install-dashjs">Install VegaTracker for Dash.js.</DocTile>
  <DocTile title="HTML5" path="/docs/streaming-video-&-ads/installation/amazon-fire-tv/install-html5">Install VegaTracker for the native HTML5 video element.</DocTile>
</DocTiles>
</Step>

<Step>
### View your data

Deploy your application and play a video. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com](https://one.newrelic.com) > Streaming Video & Ads**</DNT> to verify that data is flowing for your application.
</Step>

</Steps>
