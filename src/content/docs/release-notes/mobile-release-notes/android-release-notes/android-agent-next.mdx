## Bug fixes

* Fixed events recorded while the app was in the foreground being reported with `background = true` when they were sent in a batch harvested after the app moved to the background. With Background Reporting enabled, every event now carries `background = true` or `background = false` for the app's state at the moment it was recorded; foreground events previously had no `background` attribute. ([#655](https://github.com/newrelic/newrelic-android-agent/pull/655)) {/* short: Fixed events recorded while the app was in the foreground being reported with background = true when they were sent in a */}
* Fixed ANRs and other app exits reported on the next launch missing the `background` attribute when they happened while the app was in the background; the attribute is now `true` or `false` based on the app's state when the process exited. ([#653](https://github.com/newrelic/newrelic-android-agent/pull/653)) {/* short: Fixed ANRs and other app exits reported on the next launch missing the background attribute when they happened while the */}
