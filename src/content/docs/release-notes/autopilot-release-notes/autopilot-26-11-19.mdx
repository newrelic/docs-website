### What's new

#### Investigate with AI in Slack

Slack alert notifications now include an <DNT>**Investigate with AI**</DNT> button. Click it, and Autopilot analyzes the alert and posts its findings, such as incident triage, an issue summary, and recommended actions, directly in the notification thread.

For setup steps, see [Connect Autopilot to Slack](/docs/agentic-ai/autopilot/connect-to-slack).

### One-click Slack investigations and more precise time windows

#### New features

- **Kick off investigations in Slack with one click:** Slack alert notifications now include an <DNT>**Investigate with AI**</DNT> button. Click it, and Autopilot analyzes the alert and posts its findings, such as incident triage, an issue summary, and recommended actions, directly in the notification thread.
- **Analysis anchored to the incident:** When you ask about a past incident, Autopilot can now set its time window around when the incident opened instead of extending it to the current time.

#### Improvements & fixes

- **Links to New Relic pages:** Fixed links to other New Relic pages in Autopilot responses.
- **Infrastructure host logs:** Autopilot can now retrieve logs for infrastructure hosts by host name during investigations.
- **Time phrases:** Autopilot better understands common period phrases such as "this week," "last month," "since last week," "last day," and "recent," and distinguishes "last month" (the previous calendar month) from "past month" (the last 30 days).
- **Results for the period you ask about:** Golden metrics summaries and change event searches now focus on the window you specify, so figures and deployments outside that period are less likely to appear in the analysis.
- **Complete lists in summaries:** When you ask which items are in a given state, investigation summaries are more likely to name every item found. Autopilot keeps times you provide as written, with their time zone.
- **Entity search results:** Searches by name now return all matching entity types, with preferred types listed first. When Autopilot truncates results, it says how many matched and offers the rest.
- **Newly opened issues:** Autopilot can now retrieve alert events for an issue opened in the last few minutes.
- **Log search detail:** Answers about application logs now name the log partition and account searched.
- **Log summaries:** Autopilot now explains when there are no logs to summarize for an alert, such as when no logs fall within the time window.
- **Saved preferences:** Autopilot applies only the saved preferences that bear on your current question, such as leaving out unrelated escalation channels.
- **Resilient investigations:** Investigations are more likely to complete during deployments.
- **Message when an integration's credential expires:** When a connected MCP integration's credential expires, the banner now prompts you to reconnect the integration.
