[New Relic infrastructure](/docs/infrastructure/new-relic-infrastructure/getting-started/introduction-new-relic-infrastructure) integrations monitor the performance of popular products and services. New Relic's Google Cloud Platform (GCP) integrations let you monitor your GCP data in several New Relic features.

If you run workloads on Google Cloud, connecting GCP brings your cloud metrics into New Relic. From there, you can query the data, build dashboards, and set alerts alongside the rest of your telemetry.

## Choose how to connect [#connect]

You connect GCP to New Relic using one of three methods:

- <DNT>**Workload Identity Federation**</DNT> (recommended) — you create and own the service account, and you automatically get all available metrics for supported services, including new ones as Google adds them.
- <DNT>**New Relic service account**</DNT> — New Relic creates and manages the service account, and you get a predefined set of metrics per service.
- <DNT>**Google user account**</DNT> — credentials are tied to a Google user account, and you get a predefined set of metrics per service.

Use Workload Identity Federation for new integrations: it relies on short-lived credentials and covers more services and metrics than the other methods. Already connected with a service account or user account? That setup keeps working, and you can add new projects with this method alongside it.

For setup steps and a full comparison of the methods, see [connect your GCP service to New Relic](/docs/infrastructure/google-cloud-platform-integrations/get-started/connect-google-cloud-platform-services-new-relic).

Once connected, your GCP data reports directly to New Relic. To learn how to explore it, see [Understand your GCP data](/docs/infrastructure/google-cloud-platform-integrations/get-started/understand-your-gcp-data).
