[New Relic infrastructure](/docs/infrastructure/new-relic-infrastructure/getting-started/introduction-new-relic-infrastructure) integrations monitor the performance of popular products and services. New Relic's Google Cloud Platform (GCP) integrations let you monitor your GCP data in several New Relic features.

If you run workloads on Google Cloud, connecting GCP brings your cloud metrics into New Relic. From there, you can query the data, build dashboards, and set alerts alongside the rest of your telemetry. To start receiving GCP data, connect your Google project to New Relic's [infrastructure monitoring](/docs/infrastructure). If you don't have one already, [create a New Relic account](https://newrelic.com/signup).

For new integrations, use <DNT>**Workload Identity Federation**</DNT>. It offers better security through short-lived credentials, broader service and metric coverage, and automatic access to new metrics as GCP adds them.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Already have a service account integration? It continues to work. You can also add new projects with WIF alongside your existing setup.
</Callout>

## Setup guides [#setup-guides]

- Connect with Workload Identity Federation (recommended), using one of two options:
  - [Manually integrate your Google Cloud Platform](/docs/infrastructure/google-cloud-platform-integrations/get-started/connect-gcp-workload-identity-federation)
  - [Automate with Terraform](/docs/infrastructure/google-cloud-platform-integrations/get-started/connect-gcp-workload-identity-federation-terraform)
- [Connect with a New Relic service account](/docs/infrastructure/google-cloud-platform-integrations/get-started/connect-gcp-service-account)
- [Connect with a Google user account](/docs/infrastructure/google-cloud-platform-integrations/get-started/connect-gcp-user-account)

## Explore app data in New Relic [#find-data]

After you connect, New Relic starts monitoring your GCP data at regular polling intervals, and your data appears in the New Relic UI after a few minutes. To learn how to view, query, and explore it — including dashboards, alerts, and entities — see [Understand your GCP data](/docs/infrastructure/google-cloud-platform-integrations/get-started/understand-your-gcp-data).
