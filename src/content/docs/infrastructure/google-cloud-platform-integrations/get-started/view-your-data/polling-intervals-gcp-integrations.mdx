New Relic's GCP integrations query your GCP services according to a polling interval, which varies depending [on the integration](#manage-polling). New Relic applies the polling interval to every GCP entity.

## Polling intervals by connection method [#polling]

New Relic polls the GCP Cloud Monitoring API on a regular schedule to collect your metrics. How often this happens depends on your connection method.

## When data appears [#when-data-appears]

After you first connect a GCP project, expect data to start appearing within 2-5 minutes. Entities may take a few minutes longer to be synthesized.

For ongoing collection, data freshness depends on two factors:

- **GCP metric availability**: GCP Cloud Monitoring makes some metrics available only after a delay of up to a few minutes after sampling. This latency applies regardless of your polling interval — if you configure 1-minute polling, data for those metrics may still appear 2-3 minutes after sampling.
- **New Relic collection and processing**: After New Relic queries GCP at the configured polling interval, data typically takes an additional 1-2 minutes to appear in New Relic.
