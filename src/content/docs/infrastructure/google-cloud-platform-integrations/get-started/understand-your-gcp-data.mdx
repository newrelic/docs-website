Once you follow the configuration process, data from your Google Cloud Platform account will report directly to New Relic.

To view your GCP data:

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com > All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities)**</DNT>.
2. Search for <DNT>**Google Cloud Platform**</DNT> and click the tile.

You can view and reuse the NRQL queries both in the pre-configured <InlinePopover type="dashboards"/> and in the <DNT>**Events explorer**</DNT> dashboards. This allows you to tailor queries to your specific needs.

Events, dashboards, and other data are available for all services in New Relic.
