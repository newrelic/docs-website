<DNT>**Infrastructure 360**</DNT> brings all of your infrastructure — cloud resources, hosts, Kubernetes, and databases — together into a single, connected view. Instead of moving between separate experiences for your cloud, your hosts, and your containers, Infrastructure 360 gives you one place to see what you're running, how it's connected, how well it's monitored, and what's changing across your environment.

## Infrastructure 360 features [#features]

With Infrastructure 360, you can:

* **See all of your infrastructure in one place**: cloud resources, hosts, Kubernetes, and databases, across the New Relic accounts you have access to.
* **Auto-discover your cloud resources**: your inventory stays current as your environment changes, without having to set up metric streaming or polling first.

  <Callout variant="tip">
    Auto-discovery creates entities and relationships, not telemetry. A discovered resource gets an entity, tags, its cloud configuration, and a place on your maps — but you still need to connect metrics or install an agent to get charts and alerts for it.
  </Callout>

* **Understand your monitoring coverage at a glance**: guided nudges help you close gaps, for example by connecting a cloud account or installing an agent where a resource isn't fully instrumented.
* **See the relationships between your resources**: understand how your applications and infrastructure connect.
* **Track configuration changes**: see what changed and when across your discovered resources, with a configuration explorer and a change-tracking timeline on each entity.
* **Bring alerts, cost, and security signals into one view**: for your infrastructure.

## How it works [#how-it-works]

Infrastructure 360 discovers the resources in the cloud accounts and subscriptions you connect, and combines them with the hosts, Kubernetes clusters, and databases already monitored by New Relic. As your environment changes, discovery keeps that picture up to date and highlights where your monitoring coverage can be improved.
