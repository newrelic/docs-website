If you run into issues with [<DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT>](/docs/infrastructure/infrastructure-360/introduction-infrastructure-360) on <DNT>Azure</DNT>, review the following troubleshooting scenarios.

<CollapserGroup>
  <Collapser
    id="no-entities-appear"
    title="No Azure entities appear after enabling auto-discovery"
  >
    If no entities appear after enabling auto-discovery, check the following:

    * **Check permissions**: Ensure your <DNT>Azure</DNT> app registration has Reader permissions for the subscription and necessary resource groups.
    * **Verify subscription**: Confirm that you configured the correct <DNT>Azure</DNT> subscription ID in your New Relic integration.
    * **Wait for the first scan**: The initial discovery can take time. Check back after the next scheduled scan (default is 3 times per day).
    * **Verify preview access**: Confirm that you enrolled your New Relic account in the <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> preview by checking <DNT>**Administration > Preview & Trials**</DNT>.
  </Collapser>

  <Collapser
    id="missing-relationships"
    title="Entity relationships aren't displaying"
  >
    If relationships between entities aren't showing, check the following:

    * **Check whether the appropriate APM agent is installed**: Many relationships require you to install New Relic APM agents that actively report data from your applications.
    * **Check entity naming**: Ensure your <DNT>Azure</DNT> resources use consistent naming that allows auto-discovery to match them with APM entities.
    * **Verify network connectivity**: Confirm your <DNT>Azure</DNT> resources can reach New Relic's data collectors.
    * **Review resource tags**: Some relationships depend on proper tagging strategies in both <DNT>Azure</DNT> and your APM configuration.
  </Collapser>

  <Collapser
    id="scan-failures"
    title="Auto-discovery scans are failing"
  >
    If your auto-discovery scans consistently fail, check the following:

    * **Check API limits**: Ensure you haven't exceeded <DNT>Azure</DNT> API rate limits. Consider reducing scan frequency temporarily.
    * **Verify app registration**: Confirm your <DNT>Azure</DNT> app registration is still valid and hasn't expired.
    * **Review permissions**: Ensure the app registration still has the necessary permissions after any <DNT>Azure</DNT> policy changes.
    * **Check subscription status**: Verify your <DNT>Azure</DNT> subscription is active and accessible.
  </Collapser>
</CollapserGroup>
