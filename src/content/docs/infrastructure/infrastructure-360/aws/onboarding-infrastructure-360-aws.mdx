Before you begin, review [Requirements and supported entities](/docs/infrastructure/infrastructure-360/aws/prerequisites-infrastructure-360-aws). Access to <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> requires enrollment in the preview. Navigate to <DNT>**one.newrelic.com > Administration > Preview & Trials**</DNT>, and enroll for <DNT>**Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.

There are three ways to enable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> for <DNT>AWS</DNT>.

## Enable during AWS onboarding [#onboarding-enable]

If you're connecting <DNT>AWS</DNT> to New Relic for the first time, enable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> directly in the onboarding flow when you set up your <DNT>AWS</DNT> integration.

## Enable for an existing AWS integration [#existing-enable]

If you already have <DNT>AWS</DNT> integrated with New Relic, follow these steps:

<Steps>
<Step>

### Manage AWS integrations [#manage-aws-integrations]

To install <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> through your existing <DNT>AWS</DNT> integration:

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com](https://one.newrelic.com) > Infrastructure > AWS**</DNT>.
2. Click <DNT>**Manage AWS Integrations**</DNT>.
3. Find the account where you want to enable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT>, and click <DNT>**Install**</DNT>.
4. For <DNT>**Infrastructure 360**</DNT>, click <DNT>**Install**</DNT>, then <DNT>**Launch CloudFormation Template**</DNT>.

</Step>
<Step>

### Deploy the CloudFormation stack [#deploy-cloudformation-stack]

Deploy the <DNT>CloudFormation</DNT> template that New Relic generated:

1. On the <DNT>AWS</DNT> console, acknowledge the <DNT>CloudFormation</DNT> stack and click <DNT>**Create stack**</DNT>.
2. Wait for the stack's resources to finish creating (this can take several minutes), then go back to New Relic.

</Step>
<Step>

### Configure scan region and frequency [#select-region-and-frequency]

By default, New Relic scans all regions enabled in your account once a day. On the side panel, you can customize this:

* To scan specific regions only, select or remove regions as needed.
* To change the scan frequency, select an interval of 8, 12, or 16 hours. Minimum interval length is 8 hours. Intervals below 8 hours automatically default to 8 hours.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enable auto-discovery [#enable-auto-discovery]

On the confirmation screen, click <DNT>**Save**</DNT> to enable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> for this account.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Enable from the Infrastructure 360 page [#landing-page-enable]

To enable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> from this page:

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com](https://one.newrelic.com) > Infrastructure > Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.
2. Enable the functionality for your <DNT>AWS</DNT> provider accounts under this New Relic account.

If this page is blank (you haven't enabled <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> for any provider yet), click <DNT>**Setup Infrastructure 360**</DNT> and then enable the functionality.

## Use Infrastructure 360 [#using-infrastructure-360]

Once enabled, you can explore your <DNT>AWS</DNT> infrastructure in New Relic.

### View discovered entities and relationships [#view-entities]

After the stack is created, go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com](https://one.newrelic.com) > Infrastructure > AWS**</DNT> to view all <DNT>AWS</DNT> services from the integration step above. When a monitored application runs on <DNT>AWS</DNT> resources, you can explore relationships in New Relic maps, including relationships between <DNT>AWS</DNT> entities, between APM entities, and between <DNT>AWS</DNT> and APM entities.

### View entity configurations [#view-configuration]

To view an entity's configuration:

1. Go to <DNT>**All capabilities**</DNT> and select <DNT>**Amazon Web Services**</DNT>.
2. Select any entity from the list and click <DNT>**Configuration explorer**</DNT> to see its current settings, tags, and properties.

## Manage Infrastructure 360 settings [#manage-settings]

### Adjust scan region and frequency [#adjust-frequency]

To adjust the scanned regions or scan frequency:

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com](https://one.newrelic.com) > Infrastructure > AWS**</DNT>.
2. Click <DNT>**Manage Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.
3. Find the account you want to update, and click <DNT>**Edit**</DNT>.
4. Update the scanned regions or scan frequency, then click <DNT>**Save**</DNT>.

### Disable Infrastructure 360 [#disable]

<Callout variant="caution">
  When you disable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT>, you lose access to the entities it discovered and their configurations. The infrastructure map no longer displays relationships discovered by auto-discovery.
</Callout>

If you re-enable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT> later, it recreates any entities and relationships that no longer exist, during its next scheduled scan.

To disable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT>:

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com](https://one.newrelic.com) > Infrastructure > AWS**</DNT>.
2. Click <DNT>**Manage Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.
3. Find the account where you want to disable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT>, and click <DNT>**Edit**</DNT>.
4. On the side panel, turn off the scan.
5. Click <DNT>**Save**</DNT> to disable <DNT>Infrastructure 360</DNT>.
