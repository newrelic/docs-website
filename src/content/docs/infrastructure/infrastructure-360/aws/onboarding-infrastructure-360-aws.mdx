Before you begin, review [Requirements and supported entities](/docs/infrastructure/infrastructure-360/aws/prerequisites-infrastructure-360-aws). Access to Infrastructure 360 requires enrollment in the preview. Navigate to <DNT>**one.newrelic.com > Administration > Preview & Trials**</DNT>, and enroll for <DNT>**Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.

There are three ways to enable Infrastructure 360 for AWS.

## Enable during AWS onboarding [#onboarding-enable]

If you're connecting AWS to New Relic for the first time, enable Infrastructure 360 directly in the onboarding flow when you set up your AWS integration.

## Enable for an existing AWS integration [#existing-enable]

If you already have AWS integrated with New Relic:

<Steps>
<Step>

### Manage AWS integrations [#manage-aws-integrations]

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com > All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) > Infrastructure > AWS**</DNT>.
2. Click <DNT>**Manage AWS Integrations**</DNT>.
3. Find the account where you want to enable Infrastructure 360, and click <DNT>**Install**</DNT>.
4. For <DNT>**Infrastructure 360**</DNT>, click <DNT>**Install**</DNT>, then <DNT>**Launch CloudFormation Template**</DNT>.

</Step>
<Step>

### Deploy the CloudFormation stack [#deploy-cloudformation-stack]

1. On the AWS console, acknowledge the CloudFormation stack and click <DNT>**Create stack**</DNT>.
2. Creating the stack's resources may take several minutes. After the stack is created, go back to New Relic.

</Step>
<Step>

### Configure scan region and frequency [#select-region-and-frequency]

By default, New Relic scans all regions enabled in your account once a day. On the side panel, you can customize this:

* To scan specific regions only, select or remove regions as needed.
* To change the scan frequency, select an interval of 8 or 12 hours. Intervals below 8 hours automatically floor to 8 hours.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enable auto-discovery [#enable-auto-discovery]

On the confirmation screen, click <DNT>**Save**</DNT> to enable Infrastructure 360 for this account.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Enable from the Infrastructure 360 page [#landing-page-enable]

1. Go to <DNT>**Infrastructure > Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.
2. If you haven't enabled Infrastructure 360 for any provider yet, this page is blank. Click <DNT>**Setup Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.
3. Enable the functionality for your AWS (and Azure) provider accounts under this New Relic account.

## Use Infrastructure 360 [#using-infrastructure-360]

Once enabled, you can explore your AWS infrastructure in New Relic.

### View discovered entities and relationships [#view-entities]

After the stack is created, go to <DNT>**Infrastructure > AWS**</DNT> to view all integrated AWS services. If a New Relic service is connected to AWS entities, you can explore relationships from its map, including relationships between AWS entities, between APM entities, and between AWS and APM entities.

### View entity configurations [#view-configuration]

1. Go to <DNT>**All capabilities**</DNT> and select <DNT>**Amazon Web Services**</DNT>.
2. Select any entity from the list and click <DNT>**Configuration explorer**</DNT> to see its current settings, tags, and properties.

## Manage Infrastructure 360 settings [#manage-settings]

### Adjust scan region and frequency [#adjust-frequency]

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com > All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) > Infrastructure > AWS**</DNT>.
2. Click <DNT>**Manage Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.
3. Find the account you want to update, and click <DNT>**Edit**</DNT>.
4. Update the scanned regions or scan frequency, then click <DNT>**Save**</DNT>.

### Disable Infrastructure 360 [#disable]

<Callout variant="caution">
  When Infrastructure 360 is disabled, you lose access to the entities it discovered and their configurations. The infrastructure map no longer displays relationships discovered by auto-discovery.
</Callout>

1. Go to <DNT>**[one.newrelic.com > All capabilities](https://one.newrelic.com/all-capabilities) > Infrastructure > AWS**</DNT>.
2. Click <DNT>**Manage Infrastructure 360**</DNT>.
3. Find the account where you want to disable Infrastructure 360, and click <DNT>**Edit**</DNT>.
4. On the side panel, turn off the scan.
5. Click <DNT>**Save**</DNT> to disable Infrastructure 360.
