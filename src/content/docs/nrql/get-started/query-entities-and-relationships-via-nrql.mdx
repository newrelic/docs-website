As a [<DNT>Full platform user</DNT>](/docs/accounts/accounts-billing/new-relic-one-user-management/user-type/#user-type-capabilities), you can use **NRQL to directly query entity and relationship data in NRDB** alongside your telemetry data. This unified access supports troubleshooting and system analysis.

This document covers:

  * Querying entity and relationship data with NRQL
  * Correlating entities with telemetry for comprehensive analysis
  * Exploring practical examples, such as tracking state changes and finding application-host relationships

## Key benefits

We've centralized your data by bringing entities and their relationships directly into NRDB. This means you can use NRQL to get a complete picture of your environment, all in one place.

Here's how you benefit:

  * **Access all your entity data directly in NRDB:** NRDB ingests and stores your entity and relationship data. This creates a unified datastore, making it the central place for this critical information alongside your telemetry data.
  * **Query with ease using NRQL:** You can use the NRQL you're already familiar with to query this entity and relationship data. You don't need to learn new query languages or perform complex joins across different data stores.
  * **Get timely insights with 24-hour data availability:** NRDB retains this entity and relationship data for 24 hours, so you can perform timely analysis and retrieve the information needed to understand system behavior and identify dependencies.

<Callout variant="important">
  Entity relationship data is retained for 24 hours.
</Callout>
  * **Achieve a holistic view with simplified correlation:** You can link entities, their relationships, and your associated telemetry data (like metrics, events, logs, and traces). This correlation capability gives you a complete picture of your environment's health and performance.
