Today, we're thrilled to announce the general availability of our new OCI monitoring integration. This expansion of our infrastructure monitoring portfolio introduces out-of-the-box telemetry ingestion for OCI metrics and logs, bringing Oracle Cloud observability directly into New Relic for the first time.

With this release, multi-cloud enterprises no longer have to manage OCI in a silo. By bringing OCI metrics and logs into a single platform alongside AWS, Azure, and GCP, you can break down operational silos, eliminate multi-cloud coverage gaps, and accelerate root-cause analysis across your entire cloud footprint.

## What's new in this release

* **Native OCI Observability**: Ingest, query, and alert on performance metrics and logs from key OCI resources, including Compute, Storage, Database, and Network services.
* **Co-located Cloud Telemetry**: View and analyze your OCI performance metrics right alongside your existing AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud telemetry.
* **Multi-Cloud Troubleshooting**: Monitor and trace performance across all your cloud providers in one platform to identify and resolve cross-environment issues.

Get started with our [OCI integration guide](https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/infrastructure/oci/introduction/) and begin streaming your Oracle Cloud telemetry today!
