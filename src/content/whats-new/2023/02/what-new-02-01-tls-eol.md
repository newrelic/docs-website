Here at New Relic, security is our priority. This is why, **effective February 2, 2023**, we have updated our [Transport Layer Security (TLS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_Layer_Security) requirements for all inbound connections to a **minimum of version TLS 1.2**.

For more information check out our this **[Explorer’s Hub post](https://support.newrelic.com/s/hubtopic/aAX8W0000008dM6WAI/tls-1011-has-been-disabled-for-all-inbound-connections-on-feb-2nd-2023)**, go to our  [documentation](https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/new-relic-solutions/get-started/networks/#tls), or contact [New Relic Support](https://support.newrelic.com/s/).
